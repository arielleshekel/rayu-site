Privacy Policy

Rayu — a private space for the little moments.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

The short version. Your family's memories live on your own iPhone and, if you're signed into iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. They are never sent to us, and we cannot see them. If you share your moments, the people you invite can see them too. We collect only two small things: your email, if you choose to give it, and anonymous counts of how the app is used. No advertising, no tracking, and we never sell anything.

What Rayu stores, and where

Everything you capture in Rayu — photos, videos, audio recordings, notes, quotes, titles, the nicknames you give your family, dates and any place name you add — is stored on your device.

If you are signed into iCloud, Rayu also keeps a copy in your private iCloud database, using Apple's CloudKit. This is your own iCloud account and counts against your own iCloud storage. We have no access to it. Apple handles it under Apple's Privacy Policy.

If you are not signed into iCloud, Rayu works exactly the same, and everything stays on your iPhone only.

Sharing your moments

You can invite people to share your moments from Settings → Share. Each invite is a one-time link meant for one person, and it stops working once someone uses it.

  • Create together — they can see, add and edit moments.
  • Just enjoy — they can see moments but not change them.

When someone joins, a copy of your shared moments, including full-quality photos and videos, is kept in their iCloud and on their iPhone, through Apple's iCloud sharing. Anything they add is theirs too.

You can remove anyone at any time in Settings → Share → Manage sharing. Their phone then deletes your shared moments, and they keep only the moments they added themselves. Anyone you invited can stop sharing the same way.

What we collect about you

  • Your email, if you give it. Onboarding asks for your email. It is optional. If you give it, we keep it with your first name in Loops, our email service, to send you Rayu news and updates. Every email has an unsubscribe link. We never sell it or share it with anyone else.
  • Anonymous usage counts. Rayu uses TelemetryDeck to count things like "a moment was added" or "onboarding was finished". These counts never include your name, your email, your photos, captions or anything you write, and TelemetryDeck cannot tell who you are. They help us see what's working.

Nothing else. No advertising identifiers, no crash reporting, and no tracking across apps.

Permissions Rayu asks for

  • Camera — only when you choose to take a photo or video in Rayu. What you capture is saved in Rayu. It is not sent anywhere else.
  • Photo library — only when you choose to add an existing photo. Rayu reads the photo you pick and nothing else.
  • Microphone — only when you choose to record a voice memory. The recording is saved in Rayu. There is no transcription and no upload.
  • Location — only to add a place to a memory. When you pick a photo or video, Rayu uses the location saved inside it to name the place; otherwise it asks your iPhone where you are. Apple's geocoding service turns this into a place name, and Rayu stores only that name — for example "Brooklyn, New York". The exact coordinates are removed from photos and videos you add, so they are not stored or shared.

Every one of these is optional. Decline any of them and the rest of Rayu still works.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, Rayu schedules them on your iPhone. They may mention your family's names. They are created on your device and never sent to us.

Sharing a memory

When you share a memory, Rayu hands it to the standard iOS share sheet and you choose where it goes — Messages, WhatsApp, Mail, or anywhere else. Photos and videos you share don't include where they were taken. Rayu is not involved after that, and whatever you share is then covered by the privacy policy of the app you sent it to.

If you send us feedback

Rayu has a Feedback option in Settings. Tapping it opens a pre-filled email in your own mail app. Nothing is sent unless you press send. If you do, we receive your email address and whatever you wrote, and we use it only to read and reply to your feedback. We do not add you to any mailing list, and we do not share it with anyone.

Children

Rayu is designed for parents and caregivers to record moments about the children in their lives, and it is meant to be used by adults. We never collect information from children. Memories about them stay on your device, in your iCloud, and with the people you choose to share your moments with.

Deleting your memories and data

  • One memory — press and hold it, then choose Delete.
  • Everything on the device — delete the Rayu app.
  • The iCloud copy — on your iPhone, open Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, then remove Rayu's data.
  • Shared moments — stop sharing in Settings → Share, and the people you shared with lose their copies.
  • Your email — tap unsubscribe in any Rayu email, or write to hello@rayumoments.com and we will delete it.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data a company holds about you. The only personal data we hold is your email and first name, if you gave them, and any feedback email you chose to send us. Write to us at the address below and we will take care of it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. If a change ever means Rayu starts collecting something it does not collect today, we will update this page before it happens.
